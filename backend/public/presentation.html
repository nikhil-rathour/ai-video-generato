Problem Statement & Industry Gaps Slide 02 / 07

The High Friction & Fragmentation in Short-Form Video Creation

Vertical short-form video dominates social engagement, but creating consistent high-retention content requires excessive time, tools, and technical skills.

01. What is the Problem & Who is Affected?

  • 4–6 Hours per Video: Creators, educators, and dev advocates spend hours manually writing scripts, searching B-roll, recording voiceovers, and editing timelines.
  • Target Demographic: Social content creators, developer advocates, educators, brand marketers, and fast-paced media publishers.
  • High Tooling Costs: Subscription fragmentation across separate LLM tools, stock footage repositories, voice clones, and video editors.

02. Existing Solutions & Critical Gaps

  • Unstructured Text Output: Typical LLM tools return markdown prose with no scene timing, voice pacing, or stock queries.
  • No Unified Multi-Modal Pipeline: Traditional AI video tools require manual export/import between 5 different apps.
  • Stretched/Unsynchronized Media: Most automations produce distorted aspect ratios or mismatched voice-to-video pacing.
  • Zero Direct Publishing: Videos sit on hard drives without seamless integration into platform feeds (like Qoneqt).
Pain Point: 82% of creators abandon daily posting due to production exhaustion Qoneqt AI Studio Solution →
Proposed Solution Slide 03 / 07

One-Click Autonomous Content Engine for Qoneqt Global Feed

An end-to-end pipeline taking a single topic prompt and producing a fully composited, voice-narrated, captioned 1080x1920 vertical video with direct feed publishing.

01

Simple Concept

Input a trend/topic → Autonomous AI Agent drafts structured JSON storyboard → Scrapes matched 9:16 stock clips → Generates studio voiceover → Renders MP4 via FFmpeg → Publishes to Qoneqt.

02

How It Solves the Problem

Reduces 4+ hours of manual editing to under 30 seconds. Operates 100% autonomously with real-time SSE progress tracking across 7 distinct pipeline stages.

03

Why It's Significantly Better

Multi-Tier Resilience: Dual LLM fallback (Gemini → Groq), Dual Stock fallback (Pexels → Pixabay → Procedural), Dual TTS (ElevenLabs → Neural SAPI), and Native 1080x1920 FFmpeg rendering with zero stretched media.

System Architecture & Tech Stack Slide 04 / 07

Decoupled AI & Media Engine

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (`@google/genai`) Primary LLM

Enforces structured JSON: hooks, scene durations, voiceovers, on-screen text, and B-roll queries.

Groq Llama 3.3 70B (`groq-sdk`) Fallback LLM

Sub-second fallback inference when Gemini quota is constrained.

Pexels & Pixabay Video APIs Stock Media

Scrapes portrait (9:16) HD B-roll footage dynamically mapped to scene visual queries.

ElevenLabs & Neural SAPI / Google TTS Voice Pipeline

Synthesizes master voiceover audio with automated ffprobe duration verification.

Qoneqt Publishing (`qoneqtService.js`) Social Layer

Publishes completed 1080x1920 MP4 directly to the live Qoneqt Global Feed with sandbox demo.

100% Modular: Every AI provider can be hot-swapped without breaking the pipeline Key Features →
